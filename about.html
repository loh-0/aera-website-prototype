How We Started

Our story

More than activewear — a lifestyle.

AÉRA was born from a shared passion for movement, style and a more conscious way of living. As university students and active individuals, we found it difficult to find activewear that was both flattering and functional, while also aligning with our sustainability values.

We wanted pieces that could seamlessly take us from the gym to everyday life — without compromising on style or the planet.

A love for movement

We wanted activewear that supports every part of our day — from pilates to everyday life.

A more conscious future

We believe in doing better for people and the planet through thoughtful material choices.

By women, for women

AÉRA was created for women like us — who value comfort, confidence and sustainability without compromising on style.

What started as a personal need quickly evolved into a shared vision — to create activewear that makes you feel good, while doing good. AÉRA represents our commitment to thoughtful design, quality and a brighter tomorrow for both people and the planet.
